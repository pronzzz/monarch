**Monarch 1.0.0: your real-life workouts, as an RPG.**

Steps, training, sleep, meals and study turn into XP, five stats and levels 1–100, with a live status widget on your home screen.

### Highlights
- 🎮 **Stat window.** Level, rank, class, XP bar and five stats with daily caps and non-linear growth.
- 🔄 **Sync.** Health Connect brings in data from **Samsung Health, Zepp/Amazfit, Google Fit and Fitbit**. **Strava** can also connect directly. A workout seen by two sources only counts once.
- 🗓️ **Weekly plan.** Built on-device (or by Gemini) for your goal and schedule. It adapts to what you actually completed and can be sent to your calendar.
- 🏆 **Progress.** Daily quests, 18 achievements, classes, level-up animation, daily summary and weekly recap.
- 🛡️ **No punishment.** Rest days never break a streak, shields cover missed days, and XP is never taken away.
- 👑 **New look.** A minimal crown icon with themed-icon support.

### Install
Download `monarch-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on Android 8.0+. You'll need to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Health Connect is built into Android 14+. On older phones, the app links to it on the Play Store.

### Notes
- Gemini planning, Google Sign-In and Strava direct sync only appear in builds made with your own API keys (see the README). This APK runs fully offline, using the on-device planner and Health Connect.
- General fitness guidance only, not medical advice.
